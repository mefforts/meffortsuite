MeffortSuite 1.1.7
World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1 | Interface 16001

Combat and settings
- The Combat tile shows your equipped wand or ranged weapon on supported
  classes and your main-hand weapon otherwise. It updates when you swap
  gear and uses a sword icon if neither slot provides an icon.
- Wand and ranged controls are directly on Combat. Loot has its own tile
  in the 3x3 launcher. Settings use outlined, shadowed text and shorter
  descriptions.
- Set keybind label size, offsets, color, and effects directly in Bars.
  Reset appearance restores the default style.

Fishing
- Double right-click to cast while holding a fishing pole. When Interact Key
  is enabled, double right-click again to reel in from the active bobber.
  The client can show its native Interact cog during the cast.
- Optional fishing zoom moves into first person and tilts slightly down.
  After the cast, the view pauses for 2.75 seconds by default, then returns
  in one native zoom. Adjust the pause from 0 to 10 seconds in Fishing.
  A quick recast interrupts the return.

Tooltips and quests
- Tooltips use WoW's original descriptions, with optional header styling,
  badges, and higher-rank reminders.
- Quest dialogue hotkeys and the separate flight-master shortcut remain
  available under Quests and Map.

Removed
- Simple spell explanations and their Replace and Compare modes.
- Bars live position preview, quest reward vendor values, and automatic
  single-option service dialogue.
- Single right-click fishing and the experimental bobber highlight.
