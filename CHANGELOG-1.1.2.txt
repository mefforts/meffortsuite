MeffortSuite 1.1.2
World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1 | Interface 16001

Settings and presentation
- Use Home on settings pages to return to the launcher. Back restores the previous page and scroll position, including after the Bars preview.
- Open Bars, Combat, Map, Quests, Tooltips and About directly with /msuite commands. Use /msuite fishing settings or /msuite ranged settings for those controls.
- Keep rejected settings drafts visible with feedback. Color and range controls explain invalid entries. The loot preview stays visible above its settings window.
- Restore spell descriptions when explanations are disabled or their mode changes. Keep the Compare panel on screen and read the spell's rank from its tooltip title or rank line.

Gameplay and saved settings
- Number keys follow the native gossip order, including options identified by index. Automatic flight dialogue leaves quest choices available.
- The waypoint arrow can recover when the primary map cannot locate the player.
- Ranged and fishing controls retain native input if secure setup fails. Arcane Blast retains its estimate when the client cannot read the tracked aura.
- Manual and scheduled bank deposits take turns. Quest reward labels refresh correctly when another quest view closes.
- Quiet fishing audio restores on world transitions and respects manual volume changes. A pending defaults reset applies character settings only to the character that requested it.

Also included since the last CurseForge release
- Category tiles light up when a feature is enabled and show ON or OFF. Custom keybind labels default to size 20 with both offsets at -15. Existing label settings carry forward.
- Fast auto loot and the native backpack free-slot count are on by default. Explicit saved OFF choices carry forward.
- Bank dialogue uses the general single-option service shortcut. The flight-master shortcut has its own setting.
- Sounds includes optional pet-sound and sound-file muting. Restore Defaults includes a review before reloading, and keeps lists, macros, keybindings and other characters' settings.
- Enchanting uses the game's own controls. The old Enchant Queue is removed.
