MeffortSuite for World of Warcraft: Forever

MeffortSuite adds shortcuts and settings for quests, bags, loot, combat,
fishing, and travel. Type /msuite or open Options > AddOns > MeffortSuite
to get started.

Combat
- Configure right-click or Shift + right-click for wanding, shooting, and
  throwing on supported classes. The Combat tile follows your equipped weapon.
- Show a dot when your target is out of range. Mages can add an Arcane Blast
  stack counter and timer to Blizzard's Tracked Buffs.

Fishing
- Double right-click to cast with a fishing pole. Enable WoW's Interact Key
  and aim at the bobber to see its native cog. Double right-click again
  when it splashes.
- Optional first-person zoom tilts slightly downward during a cast. Set
  Zoom-out delay in Fishing from 0 to 10 seconds. It starts at 2.75 seconds.
- Quiet fishing audio mutes music, ambience, and dialogue while leaving
  sound effects audible.

Loot, bags, and banking
- Speed up native auto loot and show floating totals with vendor prices,
  optional Auctionator estimates, or both.
- Sort bags and your personal bank, sell junk, repair with personal gold,
  and display free bag slots on the backpack icon.
- Deposit crafting reagents automatically. Include or exclude items by name
  or item ID.

Quests and travel
- Press Space to advance quest dialogue and keys 1–9 to choose menu entries.
  Space also accepts eligible Yes/No and confirmation prompts.
- Open the flight map at recognized flight masters. Hold Shift to see the
  regular dialogue.
- Place map pins with /way X Y. Enable the optional waypoint arrow in Map
  to follow a pin.

Tooltips, bars, and sounds
- Highlight spell headers and costs, customize tooltip badges, and show a
  reminder when you've learned a higher spell rank.
- Adjust action-bar keybind labels, size, offsets, color, outline, and shadow.
- Mute native pet sounds or mute selected sounds by verified sound-file ID.

Compatibility
Built for WoW Forever 1.60.1 (Interface 16001). Auctionator is optional.
An active standalone MeffortWand takes priority for wand controls.
Spell tooltip enhancements use English client text.

For a bug report, run /msuite export and copy the diagnostic report before
reloading the UI.
