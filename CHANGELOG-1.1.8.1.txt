MeffortSuite 1.1.8.1
World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1 | Interface 16001

- Show spell costs as a whole percent of maximum mana on action icons.
  The blue label starts at the top center of the icon. Adjust its color,
  size, and offsets in Bars independently of the keybind labels.
- Group supported addon buttons under the round MeffortSuite minimap icon.
  Click it or use /msuite addons to open the menu, which includes
  MeffortSuite settings. Left-drag the icon along the rim to reposition it.
- Mana cost labels and the minimap menu start on. Existing choices carry
  forward. Turn off the minimap menu in Map to restore individual buttons.
