MeffortSuite 1.1.8.2
World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1 | Interface 16001

- Track loot in a compact coin strip below the minimap coordinates. It
  opens and starts tracking on login by default. Click the strip to expand
  or collapse the tracker. Turn off Track loot on login in Loot to start
  sessions manually.
- Review item icons, quantities, vendor values, and cached Auctionator AH
  estimates in the Items view. The Values view combines loot and coins
  into session totals. Choose Parchment, Transparent, or Blizzard styling.
- Use Reset to clear the session, or set a timer in minutes or hours beside
  Reset. The expiry alert shows the duration, item count, and values. The
  tracker keeps the final total visible until your next pickup starts a
  fresh session or you reset it.
- Fixed a Mage scroll shortcut error when Blizzard's ping interface has
  mouse focus.
