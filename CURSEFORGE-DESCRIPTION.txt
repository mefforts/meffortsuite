MeffortSuite brings quest shortcuts, bag and bank tools, loot values, combat
controls, and interface customization to World of Warcraft: Forever.
Type /msuite or open Options > AddOns > MeffortSuite to get started.

Quests and travel
- Use Space to advance quest dialogue and keys 1–9 to pick visible menu choices
  in order. Use Space to confirm eligible game prompts. Show the full vendor
  value of item rewards.
- Open recognized single-option services or flight maps. Hold Shift for normal
  dialogue. Choose your own flight destination.
- Set native map pins with /way. Turn on the optional waypoint arrow in Map
  to follow them.

Bags, bank and loot
- Sort bags and your personal bank, sell junk, repair with personal gold, and
  display the game's free-slot count on the backpack icon.
- Automatically deposit crafting reagents. Add items to deposit or keep in
  your bags by name or item ID.
- Speed up native auto loot and show floating totals using vendor values,
  optional Auctionator estimates, or both.

Combat and fishing
- Set right-click or Shift + right-click for wanding, shooting, and throwing
  on supported classes. Keep standalone MeffortWand if you already use it.
- Show a dot when a target is out of range. Mages can add an Arcane Blast stack
  counter and timer to Blizzard's Tracked Buffs.
- Fish with a single or double right-click while holding a fishing pole.
  Optional quiet fishing audio keeps the bite audible.

Tooltips, bars and sounds
- Use English spell explanations in Replace or Compare mode, colored tooltip
  badges, and a reminder when a higher spell rank is known.
- Customize action-bar keybind text and placement with a live preview.
- Mute native pet sounds or choose verified sound-file IDs to mute.

Every category shows whether its tools are enabled. Switch individual features
off or use the suite-wide switch to keep their settings saved. Settings identify
account-wide and character-specific choices. Restore Defaults offers a review
before it resets settings and reloads the UI.

For World of Warcraft: Forever (1.60.1, Interface 16001). Auctionator is optional
and used only for its cached price estimates. Spell explanations use English.
For support, run /msuite export and copy the report before reloading the UI.
